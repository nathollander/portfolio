# San Diego Lesbian Community Digital Records, Spring–Summer 2026

## Collection Overview

**Date Range:**
March 1, 2026 – August 31, 2026

**Collection Type:**
Born-digital community records

**Status:**
Completed initial preservation phase

## Project Overview

This case study documents the appraisal, capture, and preservation of publicly accessible born-digital records documenting contemporary lesbian community life in San Diego, California, between March and August 2026.

The collection includes event announcements, social media content, digital publications, event listings, and web-based community resources created by local organizations, venues, and community members. Materials were selected as examples of contemporary lesbian community infrastructure, including social spaces, cultural programming, community organizing, and digital communication practices.

The project explores the challenges of preserving community records that exist across rapidly changing digital platforms, including social media networks, third-party event platforms, interactive digital publications, and organizational websites.

## Preservation Workflow

Materials were evaluated through archival appraisal and preserved using a web archiving workflow:

* Selection and appraisal of born-digital community records
* Metadata creation and documentation
* Web capture using ArchiveWeb.page
* Preservation of web resources in WARC 1.1 format
* Replay validation using ReplayWeb.page
* Fixity verification using SHA-256 checksums
* Documentation of rights, ethical considerations, and preservation limitations

## Collection Materials

Preserved materials include:

* Community event webpages
* Instagram posts and audiovisual content
* Digital newsletters and publications
* Event listings hosted on third-party platforms
* Digital event ephemera
