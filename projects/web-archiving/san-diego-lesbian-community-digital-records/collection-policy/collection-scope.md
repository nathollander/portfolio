# Collection Scope

## Collection Title

**San Diego Lesbian Community Digital Records, Spring–Summer 2026**

## Overview

The San Diego Lesbian Community Digital Records collection documents contemporary lesbian community life in San Diego, California through publicly accessible born-digital materials created and shared between **March 1, 2026 and August 31, 2026**.

This collection explores how lesbian communities create, organize, promote, and document community activities through digital platforms. Materials represent the changing landscape of contemporary community records, where cultural events, social gatherings, educational programs, publications, and organizational activities increasingly exist through websites, social media platforms, digital publications, and third-party event services.

## Geographic Scope

Materials included in this collection document activities, organizations, venues, and community initiatives located within the municipal boundaries of **San Diego, California**.

## Community Scope

The collection focuses on materials documenting lesbian-centered community spaces, activities, and cultural production, including:

- lesbian social gatherings and community events
- cultural and artistic programming
- educational initiatives
- community organizing
- historical and archival projects
- digital publications and community media
- lesbian-centered resources and services

## Temporal Scope

The collection includes materials created, published, or documenting activities occurring between:

**March 1, 2026 – August 31, 2026**

This date range captures a six-month period of contemporary digital community activity, beginning with spring 2026 publications and extending through summer 2026 events and programming.

## Material Types

The collection includes a variety of born-digital records, including:

- social media posts and audiovisual content
- webpages
- event listings and registration pages
- digital flyers and promotional images
- interactive digital publications
- newsletters and community publications
- other publicly accessible digital materials documenting local lesbian community life

## Preservation Context

Many contemporary community records exist on platforms not designed for long-term preservation. Social media platforms, event management systems, website builders, and interactive publishing services may experience changes in availability, functionality, or content over time.

This project documents the appraisal and preservation of digital records that represent current lesbian community life while exploring challenges associated with capturing, describing, and maintaining born-digital cultural materials.

## Access and Rights

Materials included in this collection were identified from publicly accessible online sources. Preservation activities are conducted for educational and archival documentation purposes.

Copyright and intellectual property rights remain with the original creators, organizations, and platforms. Captured materials are not presented as original works of the project creator. Any future reuse, redistribution, or publication of preserved materials should consider applicable rights, permissions, and creator intent.
