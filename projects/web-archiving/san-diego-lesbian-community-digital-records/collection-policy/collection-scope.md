# Collection Scope

## Collection Title

**San Diego Lesbian Community Digital Records, Spring–Summer 2026**

## Overview

The San Diego Lesbian Community Digital Records collection documents contemporary lesbian community life in San Diego, California through publicly accessible born-digital materials created, published, or documenting activities between **March 1, 2026 and August 31, 2026**.

This collection explores how lesbian communities create, organize, promote, and preserve community activities through digital platforms. Materials represent the evolving nature of contemporary community records, where social events, cultural programming, educational initiatives, publications, and organizational activities increasingly exist through websites, social media platforms, digital publications, and third-party event services.

Materials were selected through an archival appraisal process based on their significance to documenting lesbian-centered community spaces, cultural practices, organizing efforts, and digital communication methods.

## Geographic Scope

Materials included in this collection document lesbian-centered activities, organizations, venues, and community initiatives located in or serving the San Diego, California community.

## Community Scope

The collection focuses on materials documenting lesbian-centered community spaces, activities, and cultural production, including:

* lesbian social gatherings and community events
* cultural and artistic programming
* educational initiatives
* community organizing
* historical and archival projects
* digital publications and community media
* lesbian-centered resources and services

## Temporal Scope

The collection includes materials created, published, or documenting activities occurring between:

**March 1, 2026 – August 31, 2026**

This six-month collection period captures contemporary digital community activity across spring and summer 2026, including community programming, social events, publications, and digital outreach efforts.

## Material Types

The collection includes a variety of born-digital records, including:

* social media posts and audiovisual content
* webpages
* event listings and registration pages
* digital flyers and promotional images
* interactive digital publications
* newsletters and community publications
* other publicly accessible digital materials documenting local lesbian community life

## Preservation Context

Many contemporary community records exist on platforms not designed for long-term preservation. Social media platforms, event management systems, website builders, and interactive publishing services may experience changes in availability, functionality, accessibility, or content over time.

This project documents the appraisal and preservation of digital records representing contemporary lesbian community life while exploring challenges associated with capturing, describing, validating, and maintaining born-digital cultural materials.

## Access and Rights

Materials included in this collection were identified from publicly accessible online sources. Preservation activities were conducted for educational and archival documentation purposes.

Copyright and intellectual property rights remain with original creators, organizations, and hosting platforms. Captured materials are preserved as archival records and are not claimed as original works of the project creator.

Any future reuse, redistribution, or publication of preserved materials should consider applicable copyright restrictions, platform policies, permissions, and creator intent.
