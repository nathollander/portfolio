# Selection Criteria

## Overview

This document outlines the appraisal criteria used to identify born-digital materials for inclusion in the **San Diego Lesbian Community Digital Records, Spring–Summer 2026** collection.

## Selection Criteria

Materials considered for inclusion must meet the following criteria:

### Geographic Scope

Materials must document events, activities, organizations, venues, or community initiatives occurring within the municipal boundaries of San Diego, California.

### Community Relevance

Materials must primarily document lesbian-centered community activities, spaces, organizations, or cultural production, including but not limited to:

- social gatherings
- cultural events
- educational programming
- community organizing
- archival and historical initiatives
- artistic and creative events
- community publications
- social and cultural resources

### Temporal Scope

Materials must have been created, published, or document activities occurring between **March 1, 2026 and August 31, 2026**.

### Format

Priority will be given to born-digital materials representing a range of digital formats, including:

- webpages
- social media posts and audiovisual content
- digital flyers and images
- PDFs and digital publications
- newsletters
- event listings and registration pages
- interactive web-based publications

### Preservation Value

Materials will be prioritized when they demonstrate significance for documenting contemporary lesbian community life and the challenges of preserving digital records, including:

- temporary availability or risk of disappearance
- reliance on third-party platforms
- likelihood of alteration or removal
- documentation of community activities and spaces
- evidence of contemporary lesbian cultural, social, and historical practices

## Exclusion Criteria

Materials will generally not be included if they are:

- outside San Diego city limits
- unrelated to lesbian community history, activities, or cultural production
- privately shared, restricted, or inaccessible content
- materials lacking sufficient provenance or creator information
- duplicate versions of already captured materials
- content where preservation would exceed the scope of this case study

## Appraisal Notes

Individual selection decisions will be documented separately for each preserved item through the item appraisal log. Appraisal decisions consider geographic scope, community relevance, temporal scope, format, provenance, and preservation value.
