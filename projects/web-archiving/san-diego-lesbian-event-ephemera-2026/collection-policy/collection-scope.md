# Collection Scope

## Collection Title

San Diego Lesbian Community Event Ephemera, Summer 2026

## Creator

Natalie Hollander

## Geographic Scope

This collection documents publicly accessible born-digital materials associated with lesbian community events occurring within the municipal boundaries of San Diego, California.

## Temporal Scope

Summer 2026 (dates to be determined)

## Collection Description

This digital preservation project focuses on capturing and documenting ephemeral online materials related to lesbian community events in San Diego. Materials may include digital flyers, event announcements, webpages, newsletters, community calendars, and publicly accessible social media content.

## Purpose

The purpose of this collection is to preserve examples of contemporary lesbian community life that may be vulnerable to digital loss due to changing websites, expired event pages, and the temporary nature of online event promotion.

## Scope Notes

Additional selection criteria and appraisal decisions will be documented as materials are identified.
