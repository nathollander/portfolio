# Selection Criteria

## Overview

This document outlines the appraisal criteria used to identify digital materials for inclusion in the San Diego Lesbian Community Event Ephemera, Summer 2026 collection.

## Selection Criteria

Materials considered for inclusion must meet the following criteria:

### Geographic Scope

Materials must document events, activities, or community initiatives occurring within the municipal boundaries of San Diego, California.

### Community Relevance

Materials must primarily document lesbian-centered community activities, including but not limited to:

- social gatherings
- cultural events
- educational programming
- community organizing
- fundraisers
- artistic and creative events

### Temporal Scope

Materials must relate to events occurring during Summer 2026.

### Format

Priority will be given to born-digital materials including:

- webpages
- digital flyers
- PDFs
- newsletters
- event listings
- publicly accessible social media announcements

### Preservation Value

Materials will be prioritized when they demonstrate characteristics of digital ephemera, including:

- temporary availability
- likelihood of alteration or removal
- documentation of community activities
- evidence of contemporary lesbian community life

## Exclusion Criteria

Materials will generally not be included if they are:

- outside San Diego city limits
- unrelated to lesbian community events
- privately shared or inaccessible content
- materials lacking sufficient provenance information
- duplicate versions of already captured materials

## Appraisal Notes

Individual selection decisions will be documented separately for each preserved item.
