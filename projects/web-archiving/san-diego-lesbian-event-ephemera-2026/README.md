# San Diego Lesbian Community Digital Records, Spring–Summer 2026

##Date Range

March 1, 2026 – August 31, 2026

## Project Overview

This case study documents the appraisal, capture, and preservation of publicly accessible born-digital records documenting San Diego lesbian community life between March and August 2026. The collection includes event announcements, social media content, digital publications, event listings, and web-based community resources created by local organizations, venues, and community members.

The project explores the challenges of preserving contemporary community records that exist across rapidly changing digital platforms, including social media networks, third-party event platforms, interactive publications, and organizational websites.
## Project Status

In progress.

## Preservation Focus

- Web archiving
- Born-digital ephemera
- Metadata creation
- Digital preservation workflows
- Rights and ethical considerations
