## Preservation Relationship

This digital object represents a digitized access copy derived from an original 1/2 inch videotape held by Lambda Archives of San Diego. The original physical carrier and its digital representation are treated as related but distinct preservation objects.

The original videotape contains the historical audiovisual record, while the MP4 derivative provides researcher access and supports digital discovery. Preservation documentation maintains the relationship between the physical source carrier, digitization output, descriptive metadata, and technical characteristics of the digital file.

## Format Considerations

The digital derivative is preserved as an MP4 file containing H.264 video and AAC audio streams. While MP4 is widely supported for access and playback, it is generally considered an access-oriented format rather than a preferred long-term preservation format for moving image masters.

The relationship between the original videotape carrier, digitization process, and resulting digital derivative should be maintained to support future migration or preservation actions.
